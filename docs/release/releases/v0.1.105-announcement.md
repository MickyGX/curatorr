# Curatorr v0.1.105 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.105` adds playlist backup and restore.

## Highlights

- **Back up every playlist**: export smart playlist rules, imported and static track lists, artwork, and system playlist settings to one file, then rebuild them on a new Plex install, matched against your library.
- **Keep copies of Plex playlists**: save Plex playlists that Curatorr doesn't manage without duplicating them in Plex, and restore a lost one to Plex from its card.
- **Survives a Plex rebuild**: playlist tracks are re-matched when Plex gives them new IDs, instead of being dropped.
