# Curatorr v0.1.21 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.21` is out.

## Highlights

- **Consistent playlist naming** — all Curatorr playlists now follow `Type (Username)` in Plex. Crescive, Curative, Daily Mix, global, personal, and Last.fm station playlists all get renamed in-place on first sync after upgrading. No duplicates.
- **Last.fm station playlists fixed** — two bugs from v0.1.18 meant station playlists were being created empty in Plex. Both are resolved: Recommended, Mix, and Library stations now correctly match and populate tracks from your Plex library.
- **Auto-rename on sync** — if a playlist title in Plex ever drifts from what Curatorr expects (e.g. after a format change or manual rename), Curatorr will correct it automatically on the next sync.
