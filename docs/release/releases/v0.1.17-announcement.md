# Curatorr v0.1.17 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.17` is out.

## Highlights

- **Dashboard period toggle** — Overview Stats cards now have a `7d · 30d · All` toggle in the panel header. Each card shows the selected period value with a comparison sub-line. Switching is instant — all data is pre-embedded in the page.
- **Stat card icons** — every stat card now has a large theme-coloured icon on the right with a subtle brand glow, adapting automatically to your chosen Curatorr theme colour.
- **Library Artists** — the "Curatorr at a glance" panel gains a Library Artists count: unique artists across the full track cache, including artists on compilation albums (deduplicated).
- **Playlist delta badges** — playlist artwork cards (dashboard and Playlists page) now show ↑ added / ↓ removed / = unchanged pills over the artwork, persisted per sync.
- **Last.fm dedup fix** — scrobble backfill no longer creates duplicates when Tautulli has stored artist names with Unicode hyphens or appended the artist name to the track title. The dedup window now uses the known track duration rather than a fixed 90-second window.
