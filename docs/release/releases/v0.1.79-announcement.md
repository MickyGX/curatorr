# Curatorr Release Announcement

Curatorr `0.1.79` is out.

## Highlights

- **Imported playlists are easier to manage**, with rename, refresh-period, and admin-only global sharing controls directly on the Playlists page.
- **Plex smart playlists now support artwork modes**, so you can keep auto-generated covers, preserve the current Plex artwork, or upload custom artwork that Curatorr reapplies after syncs.
- **Overview now shows live Now Playing activity**, pulling your current track and album art from active media-server sessions.
