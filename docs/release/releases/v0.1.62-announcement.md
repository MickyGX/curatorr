# Curatorr Release Announcement

Curatorr `0.1.62` is out.

Highlights:

- **Overview popups got a major polish pass** — they now use a Launcharr-style layout with better sizing, square album art, fixed artist backdrops, cleaner dates, and improved track-list scrolling.
- **Discover rows now behave properly** — artist and album entries open the right popup, album art is pulled from the correct Lidarr/manual source, and failed requests now have a usable retry button.
- **Spacing and scrolling are cleaner across the UI** — playlist, dashboard, discover, history, and sidebar layouts were tightened so panel gaps, carousel gutters, sticky headers, and auto-collapse behavior feel more consistent.
