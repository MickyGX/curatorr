# Curatorr v0.1.115 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.115` makes the Daily Mix feel fresher.

## Highlights

- **No more same favourites every other day**: the Daily Mix now remembers past mixes and leaves a favourite out for a week after it plays, so far more of your favourites get a turn.
- **You choose the spacing**: new settings in Settings → Daily Mix set how many days to wait before repeating favourites (default 7) and other tracks (default 3).
- **Always a full mix**: on smaller libraries the mix still fills up, reusing the tracks you haven't seen for longest first.
