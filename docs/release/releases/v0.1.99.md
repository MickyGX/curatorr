# Curatorr v0.1.99

## Summary

Playlist imports no longer swap in songs by the wrong artist. If a Spotify playlist included a track you didn't own, Curatorr could match it to any song in your library with the same title, often a cover or an unrelated song. Imports now only match tracks by one of the listed artists.

The Now Playing card on the Overview page also shows the play count for the track you're actually listening to. Previously it kept your all-time top track's play count, so every track seemed to have the same number of plays.

## Fixed

### Playlist import matching

- A track that names an artist now only matches a library track by one of its artists. If none is found, it is left unmatched instead of being replaced by a same-titled song, such as Jorn's cover of "Don't Stop Believin'" standing in for Journey, or The Killers' "Human" standing in for The Human League.
- Every artist listed on a track is now tried, not just the first.
- Artist names are compared more flexibly: a leading "The" is ignored, `&` and `+` are treated as "and", and joint credits in your library such as "Jacob Collier, Shawn Mendes" match either artist.
- Tracks with no artist information, such as bare M3U file entries, can still be matched by title.
- This applies to Spotify, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, and M3U `#EXTINF` metadata matching.

### Now Playing card

- The card now shows the playing track's play count rather than the all-time top track's. Counts use the same rules as Top Tracks: skipped plays are excluded, and titles and artists are matched ignoring case, extra spaces, and curly apostrophes.
- A track with no recorded plays shows "First play" instead of a count.
- When playback stops, the card restores the top track's play count along with its title, artist, and artwork.
- Fixed Now Playing album artwork on Jellyfin and Emby, which was being passed through the Plex art proxy and never loaded.
- Now Playing artwork falls back to the music icon when an image fails to load, instead of a broken image or the top track's album cover.

### Tests

- Added regression coverage for import artist matching, using the mismatches from the original report, and for the Now Playing play count.

## Upgrade Notes

- No database migration or analyzer update is required for this release.
- Existing imported playlists are not changed until they are refreshed. On the next refresh, wrong-artist matches will drop out and those tracks will appear as unmatched, so an imported playlist may get shorter.
- Some matches that previously worked by accident may now be unmatched if your library credits the track to a noticeably different artist name. Those tracks will show in the unmatched list for the import.
- The Now Playing count only includes plays Curatorr has already recorded. A track that has just started may show "First play" (or one fewer play) until its play is saved.
