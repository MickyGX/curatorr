# v0.1.72

- **Fairer Artist Pipeline scoring** — Last.fm similar artists now compete on equal footing with library artists. Compound catalog genres (Pop/Rock, R&B) expand into individual tokens so Last.fm tags like "rock" and "pop" score correctly, fixing the clustering of Last.fm suggestions at the bottom of the pipeline.
- **Browse albums & Curatorr pick** — two new pipeline actions let you either browse and choose an album yourself (loads directly into Manual Discovery with an artist card pre-filled) or let Curatorr automatically pick the best starter album and send it to Lidarr with one tap.
- **Spotify public playlist import** — shared Spotify playlist URLs can now be imported without an active Spotify OAuth connection, using the public-page scraper as a fallback.
