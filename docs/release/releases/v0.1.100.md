# Curatorr v0.1.100

## Summary

Curatorr can now count what you play through [Music Assistant](https://www.music-assistant.io/). Listening on any Music Assistant player — speakers, Chromecasts, AirPlay, Home Assistant media players — updates your stats, skips and smart playlists the same way Plex, Jellyfin or Emby plays do. It works alongside your existing media server rather than replacing it, and the Overview Now Playing card shows what's playing in Music Assistant too.

## Added

### Music Assistant play tracking

- New **Settings → Music Assistant** tab (admins only). Enter your Music Assistant address and a long-lived access token, then use **Test connection** to load your Music Assistant users and providers.
- Plays are recorded when a track finishes, is skipped, or is stopped, using your usual skip threshold. Pausing and resuming keeps a track as one play, and playing a track twice in a row counts as two plays.
- Music Assistant tracks are matched to your library through the Music Assistant provider for your media server, then by MusicBrainz recording ID, then by artist and title. A track that isn't in your library, such as one from a streaming service, still counts towards its artist.
- Each Music Assistant user is mapped to a Curatorr listener. Unmapped users are ignored, and you can choose a listener for plays with no Music Assistant user, such as playback started by a Home Assistant automation.
- The settings tab shows the connection state, how many plays have been recorded and matched, and when your token expires.

### Now Playing

- The Overview Now Playing card shows your current Music Assistant track, with its artwork and play count, when you have no Plex, Jellyfin or Emby session playing.

### Tests

- Added coverage for the Music Assistant connection, play detection (replaying events captured from a real Music Assistant 2.10 server), library matching, and the Now Playing fallback.

## Changed

- Plays from every source now go through one shared recording path. Plex, Tautulli, Jellyfin and Emby plays are recorded exactly as before.

## Upgrade Notes

- Music Assistant is off until you enable it in settings. Nothing changes for existing installs until then.
- Requires Music Assistant **2.7 or newer**, with the same Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server added as a Music Assistant provider.
- Create the token in Music Assistant under **Settings → Profile → Long-lived access tokens** while signed in as an admin, so Curatorr can list your Music Assistant users. Tokens expire after a year.
- If Music Assistant runs as a Home Assistant app, use its direct address on port `8095`, not the Home Assistant sidebar link.
- Plays made through Music Assistant are not reported by Plex or Tautulli, so they are not counted twice.
- Your Plex playlists, including the ones Curatorr creates, already appear in Music Assistant through its Plex provider, so Curatorr does not push playlists to Music Assistant separately.
- No database migration or analyzer update is required. Curatorr creates one small table the first time Music Assistant connects.
