# Curatorr v0.1.89

## Summary

- **Scheduled playlist rebuilds now target the right users.** Background jobs now discover users from stored personal playlists and generated playlists, so older or edge-case installs no longer skip playlist owners just because they are missing from play-history or completed-preference discovery.
- **Daily and weekly personal playlists are enforced by the scheduler.** Smart Playlist Sync now checks the saved rebuild schedule, skips manual playlists, respects disabled generated copies, and rebuilds due personal playlists automatically.
- **Built-in playlist failures are visible.** Rotating Playlist Sync now reports Daily Mix and Curatorr sync failures in job status instead of logging warnings while still marking the job successful.

## Fixed

- Expanded scheduler user discovery to include `user_personal_playlists` and `user_generated_playlists`.
- Smart Playlist Sync now evaluates personal playlist due windows:
  - `manual` playlists are skipped.
  - `daily` playlists rebuild after 24 hours.
  - `weekly` playlists rebuild after 7 days.
  - playlists with no previous generated build are treated as due.
- Smart Playlist Sync now records and reports personal playlist sync failures rather than swallowing them and returning a green job status.
- Rotating Playlist Sync now records and reports Daily Mix and Curatorr failures rather than hiding them behind a successful job completion.
- Added regression coverage for playlist-only users being included in scheduled job user discovery.

## Upgrade Notes

- No database migration required.
- No environment variable changes required.
- If playlists still do not update after upgrading, check **Settings -> Jobs** for both jobs:
  - **Smart Playlist Sync** handles personal smart playlists.
  - **Rotating Playlist Sync** handles Daily Mix and the built-in Curatorr playlist.
  Any sync failure should now be visible in the job status and internal logs.
