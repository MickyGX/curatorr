# Curatorr v0.1.100 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.100` adds Music Assistant as a play source: listening on any Music Assistant player now feeds your Curatorr stats and smart playlists.

## Highlights

- **Music Assistant plays count**: speakers, Chromecasts, AirPlay and Home Assistant players update your stats, skips and smart playlists, alongside Plex, Jellyfin or Emby.
- **Matched to your library**: tracks are matched through Music Assistant's Plex, Jellyfin or Emby provider, then by MusicBrainz ID, then by artist and title.
- **Now Playing follows you**: the Overview card shows your Music Assistant track when nothing is playing on your media server.
