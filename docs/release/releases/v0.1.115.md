# Curatorr v0.1.115

## Summary

This release stops the Daily Mix bringing back the same favourite songs every other day. Curatorr now keeps a history of each day's mix and spaces out repeats, so a favourite that appears today won't be back for a week by default, and many more of your favourites get a turn.

## Fixed

- **Daily Mix repeated the same favourites.** The mix avoided yesterday's tracks, but had no memory beyond that. It also always picked favourites from the same small group of your most-played tracks, and play count outweighed the daily shuffle. Your top favourites came back every other day.

  In a 14-day test with 120 favourites, the most-played favourites appeared on 7 of 14 days before this release and 2 of 14 after it. The number of different favourites used went from 54 to 95.

## Changed

- **Repeat windows for the Daily Mix.** Two new settings in **Settings → Daily Mix**:
  - **Don't repeat favourites within (days)**: default 7.
  - **Don't repeat other tracks within (days)**: default 3, for suggested and fresh tracks.

  A track inside its window is only used if the mix can't be filled otherwise. Then the tracks that appeared longest ago are reused first, and yesterday's tracks last. Set both to 0 to only avoid yesterday's mix, as before.
- **More favourites considered.** Curatorr now looks at enough favourites to cover the repeat window, and play count counts for less, so the daily shuffle can change which favourites make the mix.
- **Recently played cooldown.** The Daily Mix and Curatorr playlist setting called **Repeat cooldown** is now called **Recently played cooldown**. It works as before: tracks you listened to within that many days are less likely to be picked.

## Upgrade Notes

- Update the Curatorr app image and recreate the container as usual. The analyzer image is unchanged.
- A new `daily_mix_history` table is created automatically on the first start. No configuration changes are needed.
- The history starts with your current mix, so nothing repeats from yesterday on the day you upgrade. The full 7-day spacing builds up over the first week.
- Expect your most-played favourites about once a week instead of every other day. If your library has few favourites, the mix still fills to its full size and reuses the longest-unseen ones first.
