# Curatorr Release Announcement

Curatorr `0.1.74` is out.

## Highlights

- **Artist suggestion filtering is smarter about collaborations**, including comma-separated guest credits and person-name joint artists already present in your library.
- **Discovery recommendations are cleaner** because more temporary joint credits are suppressed before they surface as standalone artist suggestions.
- **Band-name false positives are reduced** so real act names are less likely to be filtered out by the collaboration heuristics.
