# Curatorr v0.1.110 Announcement

Curatorr `v0.1.110` counts the plays it used to miss, including tracks played offline in Plexamp.

## Highlights

- **Offline plays count**: the new Plex History Sync job reads Plex's play history and adds plays Plexamp reported after reconnecting.
- **No double counting**: plays Curatorr already has are matched and skipped, and repeated runs never add a play twice.
- **Backfill**: recover older plays from up to 365 days of Plex history with one click.
